# ECO-REVIVE
The increasing volume of municipal solid waste (MSW)
 poses a significant environmental and economic challenge,
 leading to overflowing landfills, high greenhouse 
gas emissions, and inefficient recycling processes.
 Traditional waste management systems lack real-time 
tracking, data-driven decision-making, and effective
 waste-to-energy conversion methods, resulting
 in resource wastage and pollution.


 Our goal is to revolutionize
 waste management in India
 by creating a smart
, sustainable, and citizen-
focused system. By promoting 
waste segregation at
 the source, leveraging
 AI for waste
 classification, implementing 
a reward system based on
 waste weight, ensuring 
efficient collection, and
 optimizing routes,
 we aim to establish a
 circular economy model.
